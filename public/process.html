Behind the Prediction

Click a step to explore details ↓

1
Data Engineering & EDA

Before modeling, EDA and Feature Engineering was performed. This also transformed raw financial records into features like average utilization and payment proportion ratios.

Transformation: Used make_column_transformer to scale numeric features and encode categories, ensuring the model can generalize beyond the training examples.
2
Model Benchmarking

Established a DummyClassifier baseline to measure our performance. Then, conducted a comparative analysis of Logistic Regression, SVC, Random Forest, and KNN.

Validation Strategy: Each model underwent cross-validation. Both high mean_test_score and a low std_test_score were prioritized to ensure stable performance across all folds.
3
Feature Selection

To maximize efficiency, used Recursive Feature Elimination (RFE) to reduce noise.

Note: The Logistic Regression model 70.89% fell behind our 77.88% "best guess" baseline, suggesting credit risk patterns here are too complex for a linear approach.
4
Hyperparameter Tuning

Ran Hyperparameter Optimization (Grid and Random Search) to find the ideal settings for the top-performing model.

5
Final Results

The Random Forest proved most effective, reaching 81.21% accuracy. Used RFE to narrow it down to the 23 most stable features.

• KNN: 0.7888
